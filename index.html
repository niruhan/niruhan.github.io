Niruhan Viswarupan

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at Western University, London, ON, supervised by Dr. Apurva Narayan. My research interests lie in Computer Vision, and Machine Learning.

Previously I completed an MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering at York University under Prof. Gene Cheung, and worked as a Senior Software Engineer at Citibank building counterparty default risk and credit value adjustment (CVA) systems. I hold a BSc (First Class Honours) from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka.
